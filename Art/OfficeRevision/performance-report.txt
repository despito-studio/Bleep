Submitted triangles include all rendering passes, including shadows/reflections.
They are not the unique mesh triangle count. World.Apply is a script scope; it
does not include all camera rendering. Main Thread Frame Time includes render waits.

The final build improves basement mean FPS 16.5% and p95 from 67.113 to 51.340ms,
with 25.2% fewer submitted triangles and World.Apply 1.742 -> 0.526ms/frame.
High gallery mean FPS increases 4.3%; High archive is essentially baseline (+0.3%).
Medium mean FPS increases 1.4%/4.1%, and Low archive is essentially baseline (+0.6%).

Low gallery remains a regression: mean FPS 26.201 -> 23.497 (-10.3%) and
p95 42.770 -> 88.856ms, despite median 38.224 -> 33.802ms and nearly unchanged
triangles. High/Medium p95 also remains higher in several phases. Menu geometry
is identical but mean FPS is 4.3% lower. These results do not establish that all
presets or frame-time tails improved. No direct cause for the remaining tails
was measured. Earlier repeated/probe runs also showed variable tails.

The final implementation keeps the moon at a fixed 1024 soft-shadow resolution
for both main and reflected cameras, preserves opaque roof/wall occlusion and
culls direct moon work only in the covered windowless basement. Local mirror
point/spot shadow suspension restores exact modes. Hidden furniture uses precise
cast-only bounds while structural shell/colliders remain enabled. Prop geometry
and settled door/drawer transform writes were also optimized.
